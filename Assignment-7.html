#Ljung-Box Test
Box.test(
  residuals(auto_fit),
  lag = 20,
  type = "Ljung")
## 
##  Box-Ljung test
## 
## data:  residuals(auto_fit)
## X-squared = 13.836, df = 20, p-value = 0.8387
plot.ts(residuals(auto_fit))

hist(auto_fit$residuals)

tsdiag(auto_fit)

##The Ljung-Box test produced a test statistic of 13.836 and a p-value of 0.8387, which is substantially greater than the 0.05 significance level. Therefore, we fail to reject the null hypothesis that the residuals are independently distributed across the tested lags. This indicates that there is no statistically significant evidence of remaining autocorrelation across the 20 lags examined. The result supports the conclusion that the ARIMA model has captured the main autocorrelation patterns in vehicle sales and is suitable for forecasting.
